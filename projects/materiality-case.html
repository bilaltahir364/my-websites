Double materiality assessment: Nordvorm Packaging

A worked assessment under the revised ESRS (adopted 3 July 2026) for a fictional Dutch food-packaging manufacturer, connected to the PPWR, CBAM and EUDR obligations the company faces.

Bilal Tahir · AI-assisted with a double materiality agent, judgement calls by me · November 2026

The company and every score are invented to show the method. The standards, thresholds and regulatory dates are real as of 9 November 2026.

1. Company and scope

Nordvorm (fictional: Zwolle, 1,450 employees, €540m net turnover, plants in the Netherlands, Poland and Germany) makes moulded-fibre trays, coated paperboard cartons, PET trays and aluminium-foil lids for EU food brands.

  • CSRD: in scope from FY2027. It exceeds both Omnibus I thresholds (more than €450m turnover and more than 1,000 employees).
  • CSDDD: out of scope (below 5,000 employees and €1.5bn turnover), but large customers will send due-diligence questionnaires.
  • Value-chain cap: with more than 1,000 employees, Nordvorm is not a protected undertaking and must answer customers' data requests. Its own suppliers with 1,000 or fewer employees are protected.
  • PPWR: applies since 12 August 2026, including PFAS limits for food-contact packaging.
  • CBAM: about 2,000 t a year of imported aluminium foil exceeds the 50-tonne threshold; first declaration due 30 September 2027.

2. Method

Each impact, risk and opportunity (IRO) is scored 0 to 5 on both axes. Anything at 3.0 or above on either axis is material. ESRS sets no numeric threshold, so 3.0 is a disclosed company judgement.

Impact = (scale + scope + irremediability) / 3 × likelihood / 5
Financial = magnitude × likelihood / 5
  • Top-down first: topics clearly not material were screened out at topic level, as the revised ESRS allow.
  • Whole value chain: upstream (pulp, aluminium, PET), own operations, downstream (brands, consumers, end of life).
  • Non-material means not reported: the revised ESRS require non-material information to be left out.

Not material, and why that matters

  • E3 Water and G1 Business conduct are not reported.
  • E4 Biodiversity (2.9 / 2.4) is just below threshold: reassess next year.
  • EUDR due diligence on pulp still applies from 30 December 2026, whatever the materiality result.

One data set, three uses

Supplier-specific emissions for aluminium foil serve the CBAM declaration, Scope 3 category 1 in E1-8, and customers' own CSRD and IFRS S2 data requests. Collecting it once is the cheapest compliance step here.

6. What the agent did and what I decided

  • Agent: drafted the long list per ESRS topic, proposed sub-scores with a rationale each, and mapped material topics to disclosures and laws.
  • Me: set the 3.0 threshold, decided the top-down screening, reviewed every score and checked every reference.
  • Judgement call: microplastics scoped to primary microplastics (pellet loss) only, as the revised ESRS require.
  • Judgement call: PFAS rated financially material (4.0) despite an impact score of 2.4, because the PPWR ban removes products regardless of likelihood of harm.
